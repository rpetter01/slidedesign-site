How to Animate a Chart in PowerPoint So the Bars Grow

Published November 18, 2026 · 6 min read

A chart that appears all at once is easy to miss. A chart whose bars grow one after another tells the audience where to look. In PowerPoint it takes a single effect and one setting.

Method 1: a real chart

  1. Click the chart to select it.
  2. Open the Animations tab and choose Wipe.
  3. Click Effect Options and set the direction to From Bottom. For vertical bars this makes them grow upwards.
  4. Click Effect Options again, and under Sequence choose By Category. Now each group of bars appears on its own, one after the other.

Open the Animation Pane (Animations > Animation Pane), and click the small double arrow under the effect to see one line for each bar.

Keep the axes on screen

Right-click the effect in the Animation Pane, choose Effect Options, and open the Chart Animation tab. Untick Start animation by drawing the chart background. The axes and gridlines then stay visible, and only the bars move.

Method 2: bars made from shapes

When you draw the bars yourself with rectangles, you control every one. Apply Wipe, direction From Bottom, to each bar, and then choose how they start:

StartWhat happens
On Clickthe bar waits for your click
With Previousthe bar starts at the same time as the one before it
After Previousthe bar starts when the one before it has finished

Set the first bar to On Click and the rest to After Previous, and the bars grow in a chain, one after another.

Stagger them

For a softer effect, set the bars to With Previous and give each a longer Delay in the Timing group: 0 s, 0.2 s, 0.4 s, 0.6 s. They overlap and ripple across the slide, instead of waiting for each other.

Copy the animation to the other bars

Do not repeat the settings by hand. Select the animated bar, double-click Animation Painter, and click each of the other bars. Each one gets the same effect, and you only change the delay.

Timing that feels right

  • Duration: between 0.5 and 1 second per bar. Slower feels heavy.
  • Numbers and labels: animate them with a short Fade, set to With Previous, so they arrive with their bar.
  • Horizontal bars: choose From Left instead of From Bottom.

Check it

Press Preview in the Animations tab for a quick look. Press F5 to play the whole slide the way the audience will see it. The click order matters, so test it once with the real clicks.
