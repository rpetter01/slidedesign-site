How it works

Not videos to watch. Slides to make.

Every step gives you a clear task in a PowerPoint-style editor, with the ribbon, Format Shape pane and Animation Pane. Do it, press Check, and see at once whether it worked.

1

You build it

Insert shapes, type exact sizes, align, group and animate. Nothing is premade: the slide starts empty and you make every piece.

2

It checks your work

The app looks at what you actually made, so you get feedback on the result, not on a multiple-choice guess. Stuck? Ask for a hint, or see the answer.

3

You earn your progress

XP for every step, daily streaks and levels that keep going as the course grows. Your progress is saved on your PC.

Questions

Good to know

What does it cost?

The first module is free, with no account needed. Full access to the other modules is a monthly subscription through the Microsoft Store, and you can cancel it at any time in your Microsoft account. The price is shown in the Store.

Do I need PowerPoint?

No. You practise in a PowerPoint-style editor built into the app, so nothing else is needed. The tools and techniques are the ones you use in PowerPoint.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The lessons run on your PC. A connection is only used to check your subscription.

What happens to my progress if I stop subscribing?

It stays on your PC. Only the modules that need full access are locked until you subscribe again.
